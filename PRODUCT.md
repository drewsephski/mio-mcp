# Mio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Mio is an ambient private capture system: your notes have a phone number. Phase 1 captures texts from a verified phone into a private Inbox and confirms by SMS. The existing web workspace remains available for editing, search, archiving, and attachments.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Email/password accounts, authenticated dashboard, title search, note editing and archiving, private attachments, email verification, and password recovery. Next.js App Router, TypeScript, pnpm, and Appwrite Cloud in NYC. Ownership is enforced by user sessions and Appwrite row/file permissions. SMS uses the existing Twilio provider, an Appwrite Function, TablesDB receipts and bindings, user targets, and owner-authorized Realtime. Vapi calls and unconnected-sender SMS routing are preserved. Reminders, SMS retrieval, actions, work sessions, and proactive digests remain progressive follow-ups after complete real-phone acceptance. No public sharing or team collaboration is in scope.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Mio. User-selected crisp white surfaces, compact layouts, and blue accents. User selected building directly in code.
